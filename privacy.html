Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information DevSalary ("we", "us", "the site") collects when you visit devsalary.online, how it's used, and what choices you have. DevSalary is a static informational website — we don't require accounts, logins, or payment information to use the Salary Explorer.

Information we collect

We collect a limited amount of information automatically when you visit:

  • Usage data: pages viewed, filters selected, referring site, browser type, device type, and approximate location derived from IP address, typically via analytics tools.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: small files stored in your browser, used for basic site functionality and by our advertising partner to serve and measure ads.

We do not ask for or knowingly collect your name, email address, or other personal contact details unless you choose to provide them yourself — for example, by emailing us through the Contact page.

Advertising and Google AdSense

DevSalary may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and its partner ad networks. These third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use standard web analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which pages are most visited or which browsers are common among visitors. This data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you individually.

Cookies: your choices

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies may affect how ads are shown to you, but it will not prevent you from using the Salary Explorer itself, which functions entirely client-side.

Children's privacy

DevSalary is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and third parties

We do not sell personal information. Aggregate, non-identifying analytics data may be retained by our analytics and advertising providers subject to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy & Terms for details on how AdSense partners handle data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a privacy request can be sent through our Contact page.
