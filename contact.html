What to include

  • Data corrections: the role, level, and country you're flagging, plus a source if you have one (a job posting, a labor-statistics page, etc.).
  • New role or country requests: which one, and roughly how much demand you think there is for it.
  • General questions: anything about the methodology or how a number was calculated.
  • Advertising or partnership inquiries: a short summary of what you have in mind.

Before you write in
